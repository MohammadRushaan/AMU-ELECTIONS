The Ground Agenda: Now → Then

We do not speak in vague promises. Here is what exists today, what will be tabled in the Court, and the exact administrative route to achieve it.

PILLAR 01 Core Placements & TPO

"Civil, Mechanical & Chemical Students Deserve Core Companies, Not Excuses."

⚠️ The Harsh Reality Today:

While IT firms recruit selected branches, core engineering students pay the same tuition fees only to find zero Tier-1 infrastructure recruiters on campus, forcing graduates into underpaid off-campus hustles.

✅ The Court Action Syed Will Force:

Tie central placement funding in the AMU Court to mandatory corporate outreach MoUs with giants like L&T, Tata Projects, Afcons, and PSUs, with transparent shortlisting published on public noticeboards.

Target: Core On-Campus Drives 100% Trackable
PILLAR 02 Hostels & Dining Rights

"No More Paying Arbitrary Mess Deficits Without Line-Item Accounts."

⚠️ The Harsh Reality Today:

Sudden extra mess bills hit student wallets with zero itemized auditing. Meanwhile, final-year scholars preparing for GATE, CAT, and ESE are forced into cramped 3-seater rooms without quiet study space.

✅ The Court Action Syed Will Force:

Invoke Section 22 Court inspection rights to mandate monthly balance sheets before any deficit can be debited, and guarantee seniority-based single-seater room allotments in Tarin, Morrison, and Suleiman Halls.

Target: Transparent Mess & Single Rooms Audited Monthly
PILLAR 03 NPTEL & Exam Stress

"Stop Forcing Students to Travel Off-Campus for NPTEL Exams."

⚠️ The Harsh Reality Today:

ZHCET students waste money and risk safety traveling to remote private test centers across district borders for mandatory NPTEL papers, while our own departmental computer facilities sit locked and underutilized.

✅ The Court Action Syed Will Force:

Statutory motion to certify ZHCET Computer Labs as an internal TCS iON exam nodal node, combined with a mandatory 48-hour gap between university semester theory exams.

Target: In-Campus NPTEL Testing Zero Outside Travel
PILLAR 04 Polytechnic & Evening B.E.

"Diploma and Evening Scholars Are Not Second-Class Citizens."

⚠️ The Harsh Reality Today:

University Polytechnic students lack a proper functional Boys' Canteen and modern workshop tooling. Meanwhile, Evening B.E. working scholars find administrative windows closed during their academic shift.

✅ The Court Action Syed Will Force:

Direct statutory land and budget allocation for the Boys' Polytechnic Canteen, and aligning administrative office working windows directly to 3:00 PM – 9:00 PM for B.E. scholars.

Target: Equal Faculty Dignity Polytechnic Canteen
PILLAR 05 SN & Bibi Fatima Halls

"Late Labs and Research Must Not Depend on Dark Roads."

⚠️ The Harsh Reality Today:

Women engineers working late on project simulations face poorly lit stretches and zero evening transport returning to Sarojini Naidu (SN) and Bibi Fatima Halls, restricting their academic growth.

✅ The Court Action Syed Will Force:

Mandate a scheduled evening electric shuttle loop connecting ZHCET to SN and Bibi Fatima Halls, backed by immediate Proctorial installation of solar lighting along unlit lab pathways.

Target: Safe Evening Shuttle Guaranteed Transit
PILLAR 06 Technical Teams & BAJA

"Stop Funding National Competition Hardware From Your Own Pocket."

⚠️ The Harsh Reality Today:

Engineering teams representing AMU at BAJA, Formula Student, and national robotics hackathons beg for small departmental sponsorships while labs shut at 4 PM right when prototype builds begin.

✅ The Court Action Syed Will Force:

Sanction a dedicated university innovation grant of up to ₹30,000 for verified hardware capstone prototypes, along with round-the-clock biometric lab access during project season.

Target: Prototype Reimbursements 24/7 Lab Access
